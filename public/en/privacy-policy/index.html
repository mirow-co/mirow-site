Privacy Policy

Version 2 — effective 16 August 2026. Replaces the previous version.

Mirow & Co. do Brasil Consultoria Ltda., registered under CNPJ 15.353.236/0001-89, at Rua Lauro Müller, 116, suite 1504, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil ("Mirow"), is the controller of personal data processed through https://mirow.com.br (the "Site").

This policy sets out specifically what the Site collects, why, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can object.

1. Data you give us

  • Job applications (Careers page): name, e-mail, phone, education, experience and the CV you attach. Submissions go to Mirow infrastructure on Amazon Web Services, São Paulo region, in Brazil.
  • Mirow CX Index: the answers you give the customer-experience maturity tool and any identifying details you choose to provide.
  • Direct contact: whatever you write to us by e-mail, WhatsApp or LinkedIn from the links on the Site.

2. Data collected automatically as you browse

  • Browsing data: IP address, browser and device type, language, pages viewed, time on page and traffic source.
  • Visitor company identification. We use Leadfeeder (Dealfront) to match your IP address against corporate IP range databases and infer which company the visit came from. The service does not identify you as an individual — where it succeeds, it identifies the organisation. The IP is not anonymised, because it is precisely the data that inference needs. The service also receives your browser's Google Analytics identifier.
  • Audience measurement. We use Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode v2. The advertising axis is switched off: Mirow runs no ads and no Site data feeds ad personalisation.

7. How to object to tracking

Company identification (Leadfeeder). Click the button below. The choice is stored in this browser and takes effect immediately across the whole Site.

Audience measurement. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or block cookies in your browser.

8. Your rights

Under art. 18 of the LGPD you may request: confirmation that we process your data; access to it; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed unlawfully; portability; information on who we share with; information on the consequences of refusing consent; withdrawal of consent; and objection to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these, write to contato@mirow.com.br.

Data Protection Officer (art. 41 LGPD): reachable at the same address, contato@mirow.com.br.

9. Security

We apply technical and administrative measures to protect the data, and access is restricted to authorised people who need it. No system is infallible; in the event of a security incident with relevant risk we will notify you and the Brazilian Data Protection Authority, under art. 48 of the LGPD.

The Site links to third-party pages — press outlets, social networks and partners. This policy does not apply to them.
