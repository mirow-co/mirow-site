Automotive Industry in Brazil: The Brazilian Automotive Industry in Perspective - Market, Industry, and Regulation

Brazil is currently one of the largest and most significant automotive markets in the world, supported by a broad consumer base, a well-established industrial structure, and a strong reliance on road transportation. The country brings together a complete ecosystem, ranging from the local production of vehicles and auto parts to the presence of major global automakers, suppliers, distributors, and logistics operators, along with a distinctive and complex regulatory environment.

This overview by Mirow & Co. presents an integrated view of the Brazilian automotive industry, covering the markets for passenger vehicles, trucks, buses, and the auto parts sector. The material explores the current configuration of demand, the competitive dynamics among manufacturers, the structure of the production chain, ongoing investments, and the main regulatory milestones that shape how the sector operates, offering a clear and comprehensive perspective on how the automotive industry functions in Brazil today.
