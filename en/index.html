Strategy, pricing and sourcing

We are a strategy consulting firm — and pricing and sourcing are where strategy turns into money

Strategy

We support companies across industries on their most complex strategic challenges — from market analysis and strategy design through to the implementation of the initiatives that create value.

  • Growth strategy for a pulp and paper leader, with potential revenue upside of more than 60% identified
  • New distributed generation business for an energy client, with potential of R$ 1 B in additional company valuation
Explore this practice

Pricing

We work across the commercial chain — pricing, go-to-market, customer experience and sales force effectiveness — so that price stops being a discount negotiation and becomes a management decision.

  • Pricing redesign for an international automotive company, with R$ 50 MM per year of impact on EBIT
  • Full redesign of the customer journey and experience for an LPG distributor

Sourcing

We turn sourcing into a lever of strategy: category strategy, clean sheet, renegotiation and supply chain redesign, with the savings quantified before anyone sits at the negotiation table.

  • Definition of the supply strategy for one of the largest oil and gas companies in Brazil
  • Supply chain transformation of a coatings manufacturer through S&OP and strategic sourcing, with expected impact of R$ 15 MM per year
